NETGUARD - BAN WINDOWS 64-BIT

1. Dat NetGuard.exe trong mot thu muc co quyen ghi, vi du Documents\NetGuard.
2. May dich khong can cai Python. Can Windows 10/11 64-bit.
3. Khong can cai Npcap hay driver bat goi nao khac.
   NetGuard dung API mang co san cua Windows de quet IP/MAC va kiem tra gateway.
4. Mo NetGuard.exe va chon Yes khi Windows hoi quyen Administrator.
5. Mo Mo_LichSu.cmd de chi xem lich su, sao luu va xuat bao cao,
   khong yeu cau quyen Administrator hoac Npcap.
6. Dong cua so bang X hoac Alt+F4 de thoat.

Du lieu va cau hinh duoc luu trong data\ canh NetGuard.exe.
Nhat ky nam trong logs\. Neu khong mo duoc, xem logs\startup.log
va logs\netguard.log.

EXE da gom thu vien Python, giao dien Qt, bieu tuong va font PDF tieng Viet.
Lan mo dau co the mat vai giay de giai nen thu vien.
Ban EXE nay dung giao dien; cac lenh CLI van chay tu ma nguon theo README.md.

Khi chuyen sang thu muc/may khac, chep NetGuard.exe va hai file huong dan,
Mo_LichSu.cmd. Neu muon giu lich su, chep them data\ sau khi da thoat ung dung.
Khong co lich su mang hay cau hinh ca nhan duoc nhung san trong EXE.

Giam sat ARP la kiem tra dinh ky, khong bat goi ARP thu dong.
Co the bo sot bat thuong giua cac lan kiem tra. Cache ARP cu khong duoc
dung de xac nhan thiet bi dang online. Quyen Administrator van can cho ICMP.
