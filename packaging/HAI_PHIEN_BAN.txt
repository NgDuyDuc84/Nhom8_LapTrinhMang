NETGUARD - HAI PHIEN BAN WINDOWS 64-BIT

1. NetGuard.exe: chay truc tiep, khong can Npcap.
   Quet ARP bang Windows, kiem tra gateway/bang ARP dinh ky.
   Khong nghe tung goi ARP thu dong.

2. NetGuardSetup.exe: bo cai NetGuard Full cho may demo/nguoi dung.
   - Cai NetGuardFull.exe vao thu muc ban chon va tao loi tat Desktop/Start Menu.
   - Neu chua co Npcap, tu tai Npcap 1.90 tu https://npcap.com/dist/npcap-1.90.exe.
   - Kiem tra SHA256 va chu ky Nmap truoc khi mo bo cai Npcap chinh thuc.
   - Ban can xac nhan giay phep, cac buoc cai Npcap va hop thoai quyen Windows.
   - Can Internet neu chua cai Npcap. Khong tai neu Npcap da nap duoc.
   - Neu Windows yeu cau khoi dong lai, hay khoi dong lai truoc khi mo NetGuard Full.
   - Sau khi cai, NetGuard Full quet/NGHE GOI ARP THU DONG nhu ban ban dau.
   - NetGuardFull.exe cung duoc de rieng de chay tren may da co Npcap.

Ca hai ban khong can cai Python. Khi giam sat, chon Yes neu Windows hoi quyen.
Du lieu/log luu canh file chuong trinh; --history mo lich su khong bat giam sat.
Bo cai chi thay file chuong trinh, giu nguyen data/ va logs/ dang co.

Npcap KHONG duoc nhung trong NetGuardSetup.exe, khong duoc phan phoi lai o day.
Bo cai chi tai truc tiep tu trang chinh thuc vao luc nguoi dung bam Cai dat.
Khong dung tuy chon cai im lang /S cua Npcap OEM, khong tu dong chap nhan giay phep.
Ban mien phi Npcap thuong gioi han 5 may theo giay phep cua Nmap:
https://npcap.com/#download
https://github.com/nmap/npcap/blob/master/LICENSE

De go ban Full: thoat ung dung, xoa NetGuardFull.exe va loi tat da tao.
Giu lai data/ neu muon giu lich su. Khong tu dong go Npcap vi ung dung khac co the dung chung.

Neu NetGuardSetup bao loi SHA256 sau khi Npcap thay doi file tai,
hay cap nhat ma bam tu nguon chinh thuc va build lai; khong bo qua xac minh.
